# About

Bluestick is a little desktop pal (or, "ghost/ukagaka," rather) for your pc that can do stuff! :)

"oh, what can he do?" very cool that you asked! he can talk with you, check your email, remind you of stuff, and more!

i have a lot more planned for my glorious son, but will update him when i get around to it (and have enough time/power to do so). he's currently on version 1.0 as of Feb 17th 2026.

”what's SSP?“ a super cool program that allows Bluestick and other ghosts to run!

”can SSP run on ___?“ depends! SSP is intended for Windows, but Bluestick for one was coded and ran entirely on a Macbook via the use of Wine! i'm not sure about other operating systems such as Linux, and even less sure about the possibility of running SSP or similar programs on mobile.

”how do i make my own?“ i'm not really good at explaining things, but he was made with the help of the tutorial and template at https://www.ashido.com/ukagaka/ ! it's really helpful and clear and he wouldn't exist without it! :)

”do you have any lore?“ nope! not yet. not really, anyways.

# How to Install

## -- Installing SSP

first of all, you need to download SSP, which can be done at the official site: https://ssp.shillest.net/ . find two pink boxes on the left, and click either *Complete Install Package* (the top-most download button) or *Complete Zip Package* (directly under complete full package) as pictured below:

<img width="241" height="370" alt="Screen Shot 2026-02-17 at 19 14 55" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f1fe24c8-3ad6-4d2a-82f3-b125d1338767" />

when downloading SSP, ***do not leave it in your downloads folder or a locked/protected folder.*** it's recommended to make a folder specifically for SSP, usually on your desktop. ***also do not remove the .exe file from it's folder. it has to remain within the main folder in order to access files.***

typically, on first start, SSP will boot in Japanese, which may display as simply hollow boxes depending on your system settings, or if you have the fakejapanese Winetrick enabled (Mac/Linux(?)). a pre-installed ghost named Emily is typically the first thing you'll see. to change the language, right-click on Emily to bring up the SSP menu, and select the *fourth option from the bottom*, as seen here.

<img width="352" height="102" alt="Screen Shot 2026-02-17 at 19 22 08" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f552c90b-c55a-4156-aab0-9ff09c08d2f6" />
<img width="372" height="80" alt="Screen Shot 2026-02-17 at 19 23 42" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/03f220a1-e711-4a8f-bb81-a5d4496e330f" />

note that this does *not* change the language of individual ghosts; Japanese ghosts such as Emily will remain in Japanese, while English ghosts will remain in English, and this applies for whatever other language(s) a ghost may be in.

*little tip: i recommend installing [SSP Angel](https://ukagakadreamteam.com/wiki/ghost/ssp_angel) before any other ghosts as it has really helpful tips and stuff :)*

## -- Installing the Ghost

installing an individual ghost is far simpler than installing SSP itself. all you have to do is download the *.nar* or *.zip* file and drag it onto your current ghost (which, on first use, will be Emily) to prompt an installation. after install, the install.txt file will display in a seperate window. if your current ghost is Emily, when prompted with options, click the *top option.* (in reality, i have no idea what it does.)

<img width="475" height="428" alt="Screen Shot 2026-02-17 at 19 53 01" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d7f4a768-8b9a-481b-8feb-86190ba519e1" />

<img width="340" height="176" alt="Screen Shot 2026-02-17 at 19 53 12" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a5437e16-aa15-44b7-a5e9-0b82a4c36015" />

after a successful installation, your shiny new ghost will appear alongside whichever ghost was already active. you can choose to close the old ghost through the menu if you want just your new one on the screen.

if dragging the file doesn't work, you can unzip the file using something like 7zip or The Unarchiver (Mac), and then drag the folder into the "ghost" folder in the SSP directory. if there's a balloon bundled with the ghost, drag the balloon folder into the "balloon" directory. it's reccomended to do this while SSP is closed.

## -- Installing the Balloon

i made my own balloon for Bluestick, which is included as a .zip in this repository. installing the balloon is the same process as installing a ghost. i'd recommend installing the balloon first so that the ghost it belongs to can use it on installation instead of having to switch to it afterwards. eventually i will get around to bundling Bluestick and his balloon in a single .zip, but for now, that is how it will work.

*note: Bluestick's balloon font is RockNRoll One. if you do not have the font on your computer, it will default to MS Gothic.*
